## Executive Summary

Joy is an AI-first Home Care Operating System designed to reduce administrative burden, support concierge-level care, and provide a calm, intuitive experience. This Builder Edition consolidates the approved Phase 1 direction and will be expanded into the complete engineering Product Bible.

## Approved Product Principles

- AI drafts. Humans approve.
- Enter once. Reuse everywhere.
- Every workflow autosaves.
- Graceful failure for all critical workflows.
- Technology removes friction.
- Calm before complexity.
- One source of truth.
- OpenAI is the primary AI provider with a provider abstraction layer.
- AI-guided assessment supports voice, photo, and manual entry.
- Section-by-section assessment with confidence scoring.
- Offline-first recovery and resume.
- Tap to Pay with payment fallback ladder.
