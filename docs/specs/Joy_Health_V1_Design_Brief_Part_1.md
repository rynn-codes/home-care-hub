# Joy Health V1 -- Design Brief (Part 1)

## Foundation & Operations

### Purpose

Design the foundation of Joy Health, an AI-assisted operating system for
a private duty home care agency.

This phase establishes the reusable UI framework and operational
dashboard. Do not build business logic yet.

## Product Philosophy

Joy is not: - An EMR - An HR platform - A CRM

Joy is: - An operations platform. - AI assists users but never replaces
clinical judgment. - Information is captured once and reused
everywhere. - Calm, fast, and simple.

## Brand

Primary color: #1407A2

Design goals: - Calm - Spacious - Rounded corners - Soft shadows -
Minimal clutter - Modern healthcare aesthetic

## Navigation

Sidebar: - Dashboard - Operations - Admissions - People - Scheduling -
Billing - Payroll - Reports - Settings

Top Bar: - Search - Notifications - Profile - Quick Actions

## Dashboard Philosophy

The dashboard answers one question:

"What requires my attention today?"

Do NOT create KPI-heavy dashboards.

Include: - Today's Priorities - Today's Schedule - Upcoming
Assessments - Employee Tasks - Admissions Awaiting Review - Payroll
Countdown - Billing Countdown - Compliance Alerts - Recent Activity -
Quick Actions
