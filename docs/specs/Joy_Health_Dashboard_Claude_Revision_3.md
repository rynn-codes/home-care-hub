# Joy Health Dashboard — Clean Revision Mockup for Claude
## Screen 1: Office Dashboard / Operations Command Center
### Revision 3 — Minimal, Calm, Personalized

## Revision Objective
Revise the existing Joy Health dashboard mockup. Keep the operational content and general three-column structure, but make the page substantially cleaner, calmer, and more spacious.

Use the previously provided visual references as the design standard for whitespace, typography, card simplicity, restrained color, and premium healthcare presentation.

## Personalized Morning Header
The dashboard must begin with a warm, compact personalized header.

Example:

**Good morning, Joan**

*“Small, thoughtful actions create exceptional care.”*

Display:
- greeting based on time of day
- signed-in user's preferred first name
- current day and date
- one short motivational statement
- compact local weather

Weather example:

**Houston · 92°F**  
Partly cloudy · High 96° / Low 78°

Weather requirements:
- use agency office location by default
- show current condition and temperature
- show daily high and low
- remain secondary to operational work
- do not create a large weather card
- do not show a multi-day forecast

Do not place the greeting inside a large hero banner.

## Header and Top Bar
Keep the top bar thin and quiet.

Include:
- compact universal search
- notification icon
- Quick Add button
- user avatar

Suggested search placeholder:

Search clients, caregivers, shifts...

## Visual Direction
The page should feel:
- calm
- warm
- premium
- spacious
- professional
- easy to scan

Think:
- Apple
- Linear
- Notion
- CareSwitch

Avoid:
- Salesforce density
- HubSpot-style dashboards
- excessive borders
- too many pills and badges
- too many small cards
- competing colors
- decorative gradients

Use:
- warm white or very light neutral background
- white content surfaces
- very subtle borders or shadows
- generous spacing
- rounded corners
- clear type hierarchy

## Blue Budget
Joy Royal Blue: `#1407A2`

Use blue sparingly for:
- one primary action per area
- active navigation
- selected states
- important links
- small focal accents

Most secondary actions should be white, soft gray, or text-only.

## Page Structure

Top:
1. Personalized greeting, date, motivational statement, and compact weather
2. Compact priority strip

Main Left:
1. Today's Schedule
2. Admissions

Main Center:
1. Employee Tasks
2. Compliance
3. Recent Activity

Main Right:
1. Quick Actions
2. Payroll
3. Billing
4. Upcoming Deadlines

Bottom:
1. Joy Assistant — collapsed by default

Do not use a mini calendar. Replace it with Upcoming Deadlines.

## Priority Strip
Keep six compact operational indicators:
- Open Shifts
- Assessments Today
- Orientations
- Payroll Due
- Billing Due
- Supervisor Visits

Each shows:
- label
- count or deadline
- one short supporting line only when needed

Treat the strip as one unified surface divided into six sections.

## Today's Schedule
This should be the largest and most prominent card.

Use a clean agenda, not a grid calendar.

Each row shows:
- time
- event or shift
- person
- one useful detail
- status or action only when needed

Increase row spacing and reduce metadata.

## Admissions
Show:
- New Referrals
- Phone Intakes
- Assessments Scheduled
- Pre-Onboarding
- Ready for Admission

Labels left. Numbers right. Use helper text only when it changes the next action.

## Employee Tasks
Remove progress bars.

Show:
- Orientation
- Field Orientation
- Week One Follow-Ups
- Supervisor Visits
- Annual Reviews
- Compliance Items

Example:

Supervisor Visits — 5 · 2 overdue

## Compliance
Show:
- CPR Certification
- TB Screening
- Auto Insurance
- Background Checks

Only overdue or urgent items use red or amber.

## Quick Actions
Use clean action rows or tiles:
- New Client
- New Employee
- Schedule Assessment
- Create Shift
- Run Payroll
- Billing Review

Only one primary action may use Joy Blue.

## Payroll
Show:
- Joy completion date
- latest Gusto submission date
- pending review count
- one action

Example:

**Payroll**  
Complete in Joy: Monday, Aug 10  
Latest Gusto submission: Thursday, Aug 13  
3 reviews needed  
**Review payroll**

## Billing
Show:
- invoice service period
- invoice send date
- billing exceptions
- ready-to-invoice count
- one action

Example:

**Billing**  
Invoice period: Aug 15–21  
Send by: Monday, Aug 10  
4 exceptions · 18 ready  
**Review billing audit**

## Upcoming Deadlines
Replace the mini calendar with a short list:

- Aug 10 — Payroll and invoicing
- Aug 13 — Latest Gusto submission
- Aug 14 — Payday
- Aug 18 — Office orientation

Maximum five items.

## Recent Activity
Use a quiet timeline with generous spacing and small avatars or subtle icons.

## Joy Assistant
Keep at the bottom and collapsed by default.

Title:

**Joy Assistant**

Subtitle:

Prepared for your review

Joy prepares. A human approves.

Do not show a large AI banner at the top.

## Sidebar
Navigation:
- Dashboard
- Operations
- Admissions
- People
- Scheduling
- Billing
- Payroll
- Reports
- Settings

Make the sidebar light, quiet, and spacious.

## Responsive Behavior
Desktop:
- spacious three-column layout
- Today's Schedule gets the most width

Tablet:
- two columns

Mobile:
- one column
- compact greeting, weather, and date
- accessible Quick Add
- no hover-only interactions

## Do Not Build
Do not add:
- revenue graphs
- pie charts
- marketing metrics
- executive KPI charts
- giant AI banners
- large weather cards
- multi-day weather forecast
- mini calendar
- decorative gradients
- excessive progress bars
- dense tables
- drag-and-drop customization in V1

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Personalized greeting, local weather, date, and motivational statement are visible immediately.
2. Urgent work is identifiable within five seconds.
3. Today's Schedule is the primary visual focus.
4. The page feels calm and spacious.
5. Blue is used sparingly.
6. Payroll, billing, admissions, employee follow-ups, and compliance remain visible.
7. AI is available but not dominant.
8. The design matches the clean visual references previously provided.
9. Every visible section supports an action or decision.

## Final Instruction to Claude
Revise the existing mockup rather than creating an unrelated design.

Preserve the strong operational structure, but reduce density and visual noise. Add the personalized header, daily local weather, motivational statement, simplified cards, stronger whitespace, and Joy Blue Budget.

Return one high-fidelity desktop mockup and one mobile interpretation if possible.
