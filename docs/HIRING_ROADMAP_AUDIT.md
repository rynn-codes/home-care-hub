# Hiring Screen Roadmap audit

Karynn uploaded the Hiring Screen Roadmap (UPDATED, with the 2026 superseding
addendum) on 22 August — vendored at
`docs/specs/Joy_Health_Hiring_Screen_Roadmap.md`. The built hiring flow was
made from earlier descriptions of this document; this is the check against the
print, and what the check added.

## Confirmed by the print (already built)

- **The GHL boundary, exactly as the addendum draws it.** GHL owns recruiting
  through the in-person interview; Joy's workflow begins at the human Move
  Forward decision (`invitation.ts` cites the section). The portal invitation,
  phone OTP login, and the native Joy application (Fillout-like, one question
  at a time, autosave, review, attestation) all exist.
- **One identity, no duplicates.** The candidate portal becomes the onboarding
  and then employee portal; application data builds the employee profile;
  credentials carry across the hire (`toEmployee` copies records with no
  invented dates); the first shift comes from the official Joy schedule.
- **The work queue is the screen.** Needs You | Waiting | Moving Forward, the
  candidate drawer for routine work, no Kanban, no KPI cards.
- **Candidate-facing status in friendly words, generated by Joy** — and every
  rejection reads the same, because a web page is a bad way to tell somebody
  their background check was not clear.
- **No-shows stay documented** (`no_fit` with reasons, record retained).
- **Shared documents/credentials system** — hiring reads the same credential
  requirements scheduling enforces; no hiring-only repository.
- **Human decisions.** Nothing hires, rejects, or advances anyone on its own.

## Added when the print arrived (this audit's build)

- **The `decision` stage** — "Ready for Decision" between background and
  offer, with the roadmap's gate on entering it: documents done and background
  clear. Move to Offer / Do Not Hire is a person's moment, not a drift.
- **The background vocabulary** — the roadmap's six states verbatim, with its
  rule attached: `review_required` never resolves itself; a person clears it
  or records a no-fit.
- **The `gusto_onboarding` stage** — Offer → Gusto → Orientation, in the
  roadmap's order, with `GUSTO_TRACKED_ITEMS` naming what Joy surfaces and the
  boundary comment naming what it never rebuilds.
- **The GHL event vocabulary as data** (`ghlEvents.ts`) — seven received, six
  sent, each received event mapped to its effect: document, suggest, or the
  Move Forward handoff. Effects are suggestions because a webhook is nobody's
  confirmation.
- **The drawer's portal block** — invitation, application, and "waiting on"
  (the roadmap's own example: the TB upload), derived from real state.
- **The pulse strip** — a single thin line of clickable-adjacent counts from
  the same rows the queue reads; empty stages stay off it.

## Deliberate deviations, named

- **Week 2 follow-up exists.** The roadmap's continuum ends Week 1 → Active;
  Joy checks in once more before calling somebody settled. A superset.
- **Pre-interview stages remain in the type** (`applied`, `phone_screen`) as
  history — the addendum wants the interview visible in progress views, and a
  record of where somebody came from costs nothing. The workspace's queue and
  pulse are built from post-handoff stages.
- **The pulse strip is text, not buttons, for now** — the counts sit above the
  three working filters (Hiring / Onboarding / Closed) that already slice the
  queue. Per-stage click-filtering can land with real data volumes.

## Remaining for the developer

The GHL webhook wiring itself (the vocabulary and effects are the contract),
and Gusto status sync (the port exists as `NullHrOnboardingService`; the
tracked items are named).
